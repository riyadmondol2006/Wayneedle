# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Wayneedle to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and patient. Assume good faith.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project.
* Keep discussion technical and on topic.
* Respect other people's privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, and do not publish GPS tracks
  or screenshots that reveal where someone lives or works.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
* Public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without permission.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may edit, reject or remove comments, commits, issues
and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour
they judge inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private reporting feature on this
repository (the **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**), marking the report as a conduct issue, or by
contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially and in good faith.
